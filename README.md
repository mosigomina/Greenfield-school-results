## Message to the Principal

Among all six cohorts, **JSS3A** needs the most academic assistance, recording the lowest Third Term average of 62.7 and the highest concentration of failing students, with eight learners below the pass mark. The school should prioritise **Mathematics**, as it remains the lowest-scoring subject across every single term with an overall average of only 55.4. In total, **32** students finished the academic session with a Third Term average below 50 and require targeted remedial support. Before making major instructional or promotional policy changes, we should investigate whether the high failure rates in Mathematics stem from curriculum pacing, teacher transitions, or specific test topic gaps.
